# The Cold Loop: install in 60 seconds

The part of an Execution Squad that goes at strangers. The Winning Offer, warm, talked to people
you already knew; this one builds a list of 50 named businesses you have never met, ranked by the
squad, writes the 3 messages each of them gets, stages 10 new names a day for your own hand, keeps
the log that says what actually went out, and on Sunday reads 3 numbers off finished batches and
hands you the Improve line: one change, or a named hold. Once one batch has gone out by your hand,
it puts the same send on Instantly, so the sending happens while you sleep, from your own mailbox,
at the daily number you already sent, on a list you said go to.

It never sends anything. You press send, or you say the word.

## What to bring

Your offer document at `squad/business.md` (the draft from your warm calls is enough to start).
A town and a trade. **A real postal address**, because every cold message carries one by law, at any
volume, with no exception for business email. The mailbox these go out of, which is never the
address your paying clients reply to.

## Install

Drop this whole folder, `references/` included, into `.claude/skills/` in your business folder, then
quit and reopen Claude Code. One tool to start: **Apify**, wired with your own token the way the
Winning Offer lesson wired it, which is what pulls the list. Already wired there, and there is
nothing to do. Instantly comes after your first batch has gone out by hand, so there is nothing to
buy today.

## Run it

The sentences, in the order the week runs. Monday is the list; the sending days are the send;
Sunday is the read:

- **"Build my outreach list."** Monday. It asks your town, your country and the list's country,
  then pulls 120 businesses, drops the chains and the dead rows, drops everyone you have already
  written to, writes one sentence per company on what is broken there, ranks the rows by how sharp
  that sentence is, keeps the top 50, and prints them numbered. You read the list and say **go**, or
  **swap row N**.
- **"Write my cold email."** One message under 125 words whose first line is that company's broken
  thing and whose one ask is a call, plus the day-4 and day-9 follow-ups, each with your address and
  an opt-out on it. You change any word that is not yours.
- **"Send this week's batch."** Every sending morning it stages at most 10 new names, plus the
  names due their day-4 and day-9 follow-ups, as drafts in your mailbox or as one file to copy. The
  50 enter over 5 days. You read the first lines and press send. It refuses to stage more than 10
  new names a day, and says why in one line. If Gmail flags the mailbox, a second free Gmail takes
  over.
- **"This week's batch went out."** Stamps what actually left, and prints the week's rows in your
  Outreach Sheet's 7 Cold-tab columns for you to paste. The Status cell is yours the day a reply or
  a call lands.
- **"Put the send on Instantly."** Once one batch has fully gone out by your hand. It connects your
  Instantly, builds one campaign paused with your 3 messages at the daily number you sent by hand,
  and waits. **"Turn it on."** starts it. From then on **"Send this week's batch."** loads the week's
  50 into the running campaign, after a DNS check and a bounce check. No Instantly is fine: 10 a day
  by hand is the same lane.
- **"Read my batch."** Sunday, before you write your post. 3 numbers off batches that have finished:
  people contacted, replies, calls booked. Under 300 people, 3 against 5 is a coin, and it says so
  rather than let you rewrite a working email on noise. Then the Improve line, one change or a named
  hold, and the sentence to paste into your 4-week plan and your Sunday post. The day money clears
  from this list, it writes that batch's first line down as the winner, with nothing for you to do.
- **"Raise the daily number."**, **"Add a second town."**, **"Put the read on a routine."** The
  routine, and it opens on one signal: a batch whose first line booked 2 or more calls, or one paying
  client from this list. Three moves, one at a time: a second mailbox (an alias first, a second domain
  only when the first is full, with its 14-day wait), a second town or trade on the same first line,
  and the Sunday read on a local routine on your laptop. Replies are never automated.

Stopped halfway, or closed the laptop? Say **"continue the cold loop"** in a new window. It reads
what is on disk and picks up at the first thing missing.

## The 2 gates, in one line each

**Instantly** opens on one finished batch by your hand. **The routine** opens on 2 calls off one
first line, or one paying client. Both are counts, and the skill prints which one it read.

## What you get

One ranked list a week you would defend to a stranger, 3 messages in your own voice with a footer
that keeps you legal, a log that drops everyone you already wrote to so you never email a town
twice, the week's rows for your sheet, a Sunday read that measures what finished and names one
change or one hold, and once your hand has proven the first batch, one campaign sending overnight on
Instantly's servers while you sleep. Replies come to you, and you answer them yourself. That part
never gets automated.
