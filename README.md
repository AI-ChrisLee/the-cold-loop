# The Cold Loop: install in 60 seconds

The part of an Execution Squad that goes at strangers. Warm Extract talked to people you already
knew; this one builds a list of 50 named businesses you have never met, writes the 3 messages each
of them gets, keeps the log that says what actually went out, and on Sunday hands you 2 numbers and
one change. Once you have finished a batch by hand, it loads the next one into Instantly so the
sending happens while you sleep, from your own mailbox, on a list you personally approved.

It never sends anything. You press send.

## What to bring

Your offer document at `squad/business.md` (the draft from your warm calls is enough to start).
A town and a trade. **A real postal address**, because every cold message carries one by law, at any
volume, with no exception for business email. The mailbox these go out of, which is never the
address your paying clients reply to. And 30 minutes a day, which is what 50 people a week actually
costs once the follow-ups stack.

## Install

Drop this whole folder, `references/` included, into `.claude/skills/` in your business folder, then
quit and reopen Claude Code. One tool to start: **Apify**, wired with your own token the way the
Winning Offer lesson wired it, which is what pulls the list. Already wired there, and there is
nothing to do. Instantly comes later and only after your first batch is finished, so there is
nothing to buy today.

## Run it

Four sentences, in the order the weeks happen:

- **"Build my outreach list."** It asks your town, your country and the list's country, then pulls
  120 businesses, drops the chains and the dead rows, writes one sentence per company on what is
  broken there, and stops. You cut it to 50 and say why. Your reason goes in the file, and the next
  pull reads it back and works to it.
- **"Write my cold email."** One message under 125 words whose first line is that company's broken
  thing, plus the day-4 and day-9 follow-ups, each with your address and an opt-out on it. You
  change any word that is not yours.
- **"Send this week's batch."** Every morning it prints all 3 cohorts, today's 10 new names plus the
  2 follow-up groups, as drafts in your mailbox or as one file to copy. You read the first lines and
  press send. At the end of the week, **"this week's batch went out."** stamps what actually left.
- **"Read my batch."** Sunday, before you write your post. Two numbers off finished batches, the
  honest range in plain words, the stage that broke, one change for next week, and a scoreboard
  showing exactly which conditions still stand between you and the next step.

Then, once the scoreboard says so: **"Put the send on Instantly."** It builds one campaign, leaves it
paused, loads the 50 you just approved, and waits. **"Turn it on."** starts it. Same mailbox, same
daily number, same list you approved. It multiplies nothing; it removes the copy and paste. Every
week after that, your cut is still the thing the load waits on.

Stopped halfway, or closed the laptop? Say **"continue the cold loop"** in a new window. It reads
what is on disk and picks up at the first thing missing.

## The 2 gates, and why they are there

**Gate A** opens the Instantly send: one finished batch, and you approving the list by hand. It
usually clears in about 3 weeks and it costs nothing to reach.

**Gate B** opens everything that scales: a second domain, a higher daily number, a second town, any
split test. It asks for one client paid from this list, 2 calls booked across 2 different batches,
100 people contacted, and the winning list cut written down at the moment the money landed. Three of
those 4 are your own word, and the skill says so every time it prints them.

Neither gate is a reply rate. At 50 people a week, 3 replies against 4 is a coin, and the skill will
tell you that in words rather than let you rewrite a working email on noise.

## What you get

One list a week you would defend to a stranger, 3 messages in your own voice with a footer that
keeps you legal, a log that dedups every future pull so you never email a town twice, a Sunday read
that names one thing to change, and eventually one campaign sending overnight on Instantly's servers
while you sleep. Replies come to you, and you answer them yourself. That part never gets automated.
