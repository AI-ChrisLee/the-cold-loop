# The Cold Loop: install in 60 seconds

This skill is a base. Once you have done it your way, tell your squad "update the skill to do it
like this."

The part of an Execution Squad that goes at strangers: 50 named businesses you have never met, each
row carrying the one thing broken there, the 3 messages they get, 10 new names a morning out of
your own mailbox, and a Sunday read off finished batches.

It never sends anything. You press send, or you say the word.

## What to bring

Your offer document at `squad/business.md` (the draft from your warm calls is enough). A town and a
trade. **A real postal address**, because every cold message carries one by law, at any volume,
with no exception for business email. The mailbox these leave from, never the one your paying
clients reply to.

## Install

Drop this whole folder, `references/` included, into `.claude/skills/` in your business folder,
then quit and reopen Claude Code. One tool to start: **Apify**, wired with your own token the way
the Winning Offer lesson wired it. Instantly comes after your first batch, so there is nothing to
buy today.

## Run it

- **"Build my outreach list."** Monday. It asks your town, both countries and your postal address,
  pulls 120 businesses for about 48 cents, and drops the chains, the dead rows and everyone you
  already wrote to. Back come the top 50 ranked, one sentence each. Say **go**, or **swap row N**.
- **"Write my cold email."** It asks which mailbox these leave from. Back come 3 messages, day 1,
  day 4, day 9, your address and an opt-out under each. Change any word that is not yours.
- **"Send this week's batch."** Every sending morning: up to 10 new names plus the follow-ups due,
  as drafts or one file to copy. You read the first lines and press send.
- **"This week's batch went out."** Stamps the log, `squad/outreach-sent.md`, so the next list
  drops everyone you already wrote to.
- **"Put the send on Instantly."** Once one batch has fully gone out by your hand. One campaign,
  built paused. **"Turn it on."** starts it, and nothing else does. No Instantly is fine: 10 a day
  by hand is the same lane.
- **"Read my batch."** Sunday, before your post. 3 numbers off a finished batch: people contacted,
  replies, calls booked. Then one line for next week, a change or a hold.
- **"Raise the daily number."**, **"Add a second town."**, **"Put the read on a routine."** The
  routine, on one count: a batch whose first line booked 2 or more calls, or one paying client from
  this list. One move a week, never 2. Replies are never automated.

Stopped halfway, or closed the laptop? Say **"continue the cold loop"** in a new window. It reads
what is on disk and picks up at the first thing missing.

## What you get

One ranked list a week you would defend to a stranger, 3 messages in your own voice with a footer
that keeps you legal, a log that stops you emailing a town twice, a Sunday read that names one
change or one hold, and a campaign sending overnight once your hand proved the first batch. Replies
come to you, and you answer them yourself.
